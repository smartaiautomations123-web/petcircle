Apple Store Description (4000 max)
Never wonder if the dog was fed, walked, or given medication again. PetCircle keeps families, dog walkers, and pet sitters in complete coordination. Whether managing daily routines across a busy household or handing off care to a weekend sitter, PetCircle ensures every meal, medication, walk, and vet note is logged clearly and shared instantly. Built local-first and privacy-minded: start using PetCircle immediately with zero forced logins or cloud accounts. When you're ready to share, invite family and carers with secure, expiring access codes. LIVE TODAY DASHBOARD & ROUTINES • See at a glance whether today's breakfast, dinner, or lunchtime walk has been completed • One-tap quick logging for meals, water refreshes, potty breaks, medications, and training sessions • Clean timeline feed showing who completed each activity and when SHARED CARE CIRCLE & SITTER PASS • Invite household members, partners, and family to co-manage pets seamlessly • Generate temporary Sitter Passes with read & log access for dog walkers and weekend boarders • Automatically protect sensitive information: insurance numbers, vet microchips, and home notes stay private from temporary sitters MEDICATION & DOSING PROTOCOLS • Log exact dosages, schedules, and administration times • Prevent dangerous double-dosing across family members • Clear instructions attached to each prescription for sitters WEIGHT TRACKING & CLINICAL RECORDS • Record veterinary visits, clinical observations, and diagnosis notes • Track weight trends over time with visual growth curves • Store booster vaccination due dates, worming schedules, and flea prevention alerts OFFLINE-FIRST ARCHITECTURE • 100% functional without cellular signal or Wi-Fi (park walks, remote trails, or clinic basements) • Automatic background synchronisation when connectivity returns • No account registration required to start logging Designed and supported by Smart AI Automations Ltd.
