3. Fact-locked product copy

- Local-first architecture: PetCircle works completely offline without requiring any account creation or cloud connection. All pet profiles, meal logs, routines, and safety documents live in secure device sandbox storage.
- Optional shared circles: Users can create an optional cloud account powered by Supabase to invite family members, dog walkers, and sitters to share real-time updates across multiple iOS and Android devices.
- Today routine tracker: Tracks daily breakfast, dinner, medications, walks, and grooming with configurable grace periods to prevent accidental double-feeding and alert carers when care is overdue.
- Care activity feed: Logs meals (grams & food type), walks (duration, waste notes), medications (dosage, administration timestamp, next due), and observations with carer attribution.
- Sitter Pass & Handover: Create time-limited sitter passes with automatic expiry. Sensitive records (insurance policy details and microchip numbers) are automatically withheld from sitter view.
- Emergency Kit & Lost Pet Poster: Stores 24-hour vet clinic contacts, microchip ID, and insurance policies, and generates an instant, high-resolution printable A4 lost pet poster.
- Exclusions: No fake ratings, no invented testimonials, no medical diagnostics guarantees.
