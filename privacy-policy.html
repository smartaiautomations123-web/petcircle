Privacy Policy for PetCircle

PetCircle ("we", "our", or "us"), published by Smart AI Automations Ltd, provides a mobile application designed for families, pet owners, dog walkers, and sitters to coordinate pet care, daily routines, health records, and emergency information.

1. Data Controller & Contact

The UK GDPR and Data Protection Data Controller responsible for PetCircle is:

Smart AI Automations Ltd
Email: hello@smartaiautomations.co.uk
United Kingdom

2. Overview & Local-First Principles

PetCircle operates on a local-first architecture. When you install PetCircle, all pet profiles, feeding logs, routine checklists, weight entries, and emergency documents are stored securely in your physical device's private sandbox storage. No account is required to use the full application.

If you choose to create a shared circle to coordinate across multiple devices, you may optionally sign up with an email and password to sync records via encrypted cloud database services (Supabase). Sample records and private device data are never uploaded automatically.

3. Permissions & Device Access

  • Photo Library: Used solely when you choose to attach a photo for your pet's profile or for generating an emergency lost pet poster. We use the Android Photo Picker and iOS system picker to access only the specific images you select.
  • Camera: Used only if you choose to take a photo of your pet directly within the app.
  • Notifications: Used only on your local device to remind you of overdue feeding routines, scheduled walks, and veterinary appointments. No promotional notifications are sent.
  • Local Storage: Used to save local pet care entries, offline databases, and settings on your device.

4. Sensitive Information & Sitter Privacy

PetCircle includes specialized privacy protection for shared circles. When you invite a temporary pet sitter or dog walker with an expiring access pass, sensitive fields such as your pet's microchip number, insurance policy ID, and full veterinary clinical notes are withheld from their view.

5. No Tracking & No Third-Party Advertising

  • PetCircle does not track or store your background GPS location.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with data brokers or third parties.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements or embed ad attribution SDKs.

6. Account & Data Deletion (Right to Erasure)

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Local Device Data: You can clear all stored device records at any time from in-app Settings, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Cloud Accounts: If you created an optional cloud account for shared circles, you can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud circles directly inside the app under Settings → Delete Account, or by visiting https://petcircle.app/account-deletion or emailing hello@smartaiautomations.co.uk.

7. Security

All network communications use industry-standard HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.3). Access tokens are stored securely in iOS Keychain and Android EncryptedSharedPreferences (via Expo SecureStore).
