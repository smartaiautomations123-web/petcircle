Description (4000 chars max):
Never wonder if the dog was fed, walked, or given medication again.

PetCircle keeps families, dog walkers, and pet sitters in complete coordination. Whether managing daily routines across a busy household or handing off care to a weekend sitter, PetCircle ensures every meal, medication, walk, and vet note is logged clearly and shared instantly.

Built local-first and privacy-minded: start using PetCircle immediately with zero forced logins or cloud accounts. When you're ready to share, invite family and carers with secure, expiring access codes.

LIVE TODAY DASHBOARD & ROUTINES
• See at a glance whether today's breakfast, dinner, or lunchtime walk has been completed
• Custom daily routines for meals, exercise, training, grooming, and medications
• Built-in grace periods prevent accidental double-feeding and alert you when care is overdue
• Supports multiple pets: easily toggle between dogs, cats, and small animals

SHARED CARE LOG & ACTIVITY FEED
• Log feeding times, food types, and exact gram portions
• Record walks with duration, route notes, and bathroom updates
• Track medications with dosage, administration times, and next-dose alerts
• Carer attribution shows exactly who logged each event and when

PET HEALTH, VET & WEIGHT TRACKING
• Log weight check-ins and view clear visual weight trend graphs
• Record vaccination records, booster reminders, and vet appointment notes
• Track symptoms, allergies, and dietary reactions with date-stamped notes
• Keep microchip numbers, pet insurance policy IDs, and emergency clinic phone numbers in one tap

SECURE SITTER PASS & HANDOVER CARDS
• Going away for the weekend? Create a secure Sitter Pass with a custom expiry date
• Handover view presents essential routines, feeding portions, vet contacts, and house rules
• Sensitive records (insurance policy details and microchip numbers) stay private
• Revoke carer access anytime in one tap

EMERGENCY KIT & LOST PET POSTER
• Emergency contacts and 24-hour vet hospital access ready when seconds count
• Instant Lost Pet Poster generator formats a high-resolution, printable A4 poster with your pet's photo, identifying markings, microchip ID, and emergency contact

100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE READY
• Works entirely offline on your device — perfect for walks in rural areas and basements
• No mandatory accounts, zero advertisements, and zero third-party tracking
• Clear and export your pet's complete history anytime
